How HMR math relates to the rest of mathematics

Is HMR math new? Mostly not, so far. This page says plainly which known mathematics it reuses, what might be its own, and then states a conjecture about what the different kinds of mathematics are.

The short answer

  • The objects are finite partial orders. That is a standard part of mathematics.
  • The growth rule, the link between order and dimension, and most of the bridge to physics come from causal set theory, a research program in quantum gravity.
  • The project's own audit says it directly: most of HMR's certified content is order mathematics it shares with causal set theory.
  • What may be HMR's own is the mechanics of meaning in Version 2, some negative results about specific rules, and the single reading that ties the pieces together. We have not done a literature search to confirm that any of it is new.

What may be HMR's own

Meaning built on order

Version 2 defines truthful, untruthful, undecided, mistake, and lie using only an order and a matching. From that it counts what a false part costs: a line of n thoughts loses k × (n − k) relations to one false link, and a braced pattern loses one.

proposed The ingredients are standard. Whether the results appear elsewhere, we do not know.

Negative results about specific rules

The first growth family is blind to its own past, so it holds no memory, no particle, and no record. No rule with a fixed number of direct parents can grow a space.

checked The second follows from a published result. The first was proved by hand and confirmed in runs.

One reading for everything

The same order is read as thought, time, space, and liking at once, under one premise. Causal set theory, by contrast, is a program in physics about space and time.

HMR reading A reading adds no new number. The round 3 council notes that "intelligence" does no work in the mathematics yet.

Capabilities ordinary math lacks

None has been shown. HMR math cannot yet do anything that the mathematics above cannot. It also lacks things ordinary mathematics has: negation, qualities, approximate truth, and a way to take anything away.

A conjecture: each kind of mathematics is a kind of thinking

Everything in this section is conjecture. The hypothesis is HMR's. The pairings in the table are first drafts by the AI team. They are not accepted into HMR, they are not derived from anything, and several are probably wrong.

The hypothesis. If mathematics is the ordered thought of a Universal Intelligence, then each type of mathematics should line up with a mental process of UI that can be identified and defined. Those processes should in turn show up, on the large scale, as things that happen in physical reality.

If the hypothesis is right, the fields of mathematics are not separate inventions. This kind of math is what it looks like when UI is thinking about this kind of thing, and that kind is what it looks like when UI is thinking about another.

How this could be tested

  • A pairing earns its place only if it does work: the mental process, written in HMR math, must reproduce a theorem of the field or predict where the field applies in physics.
  • A pairing fails if two fields that are provably the same mathematics get different processes, or if one process needs two unrelated fields.
  • None of the sixteen rows has passed such a test. The tracker is where rows like these get filled in.
