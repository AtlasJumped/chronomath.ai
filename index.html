HMR math and HMR physics

The math and mechanics of Universal Intelligence

This site has one job. It works out the mathematics of the Holistic Model of Reality (HMR) in the open, and it shows a human reader exactly where the human idea ends and where AI is running ahead.

See where it runs away Start with the primer

Either there is a Universal Intelligence, or there is not

That is a yes or no fact about reality. Nobody gets a third option.

If there is not

Matter is the bottom layer. Materialism holds, and physics carries on with the work it is already doing. This site has nothing to add to that case.

If there is

Then nobody has a usable model of it. There is no mathematics a person can pick up and use to think about it, test it, or build on it.

This site works only on the second case. It rests on one premise and one claim.

  • The premise: a Universal Intelligence (UI) exists.
  • The claim: if it exists, it can be reverse engineered. Physical reality is what it made, and what physics has measured is the evidence.

The premise is HMR's. It is not an established result, and nothing on this site proves it. What the site does is reason forward from it and label every step by how firmly it stands.

The core of HMR in three sentences

  1. A Universal Intelligence exists.
  2. Before the Big Bang it was unlimited.
  3. The Big Bang is the manifestation of a system in which that Universal Intelligence imposes limits on itself.

These are the only premises. Everything else on this site is either built on them or labeled as something more. Source: universemodel.ai.

The God question

Explain and define the mechanics by which an unlimited Universal Intelligence created a reality in which it is limited.

When people ask HMR about God, this is the specific question it points to. If the premise is accepted, the problem of God becomes a problem of mechanics: what the self-imposed limits were, and why.

One part of it is especially hard. To explain anything, a perspective must be established first. How can a perspective exist before space and time?

open HMR has not solved it. The latest attempt is on the open problems page.

Where the work stands, in five lines

  • The mathematics has one object, a thought, and one operation, thinking one. Version 2 is dated November 23, 2026.
  • It has exact results about order, truth, and what one false part costs an idea, checked by computer on every small pattern.
  • It has two negative results: the first family of growth rules cannot remember, and no rule tested so far holds a number of dimensions.
  • It predicts no number that physics lacks, and it has made no progress on any famous open problem.
  • Most of its certified content is order mathematics that it shares with causal set theory, a published research program in physics.

Those five lines are taken from the live math and physics pages and from the project's own audit in the HMR Math repository.
