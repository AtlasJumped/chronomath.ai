One row is one thing seen three ways: what UI is doing, the mathematics and mechanics of that, and what it becomes in the physical world. A filled cell in one column is a clue for the empty cells beside it.

How to read it

Rows from science

The Big Bang and its stages are measured in numbers, and science has its own account. The physics column is full. The work is to fill in the first column.

Rows from HMR

These start from something HMR says UI does. The work is to find the mathematics, and then what it is in physics.

One column only

Something known in one language with nothing yet in the other two. These are the widest gaps.

Status of each cell. sourced stated by a source: published science, or HMR's own pages for the HMR column. derived proved, counted, or measured in runs within HMR math. conjecture proposed, or a reading that adds no number. empty nothing yet.

Sourced does not mean true. In the HMR column it means only that HMR says it. The numbers for the Big Bang epochs come from two teaching sources that we opened, the University of Northern Iowa astronomy course notes and the Las Cumbres Observatory early universe page. They are rounded, and the two sources do not always agree. Where they differ, the cell says so.
