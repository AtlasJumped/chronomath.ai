Open problems

What is not done

Each problem has an approach, the progress so far, and a status. The statuses are our own judgment. A reframing does not count as progress.

The three statuses

  • making progress There is a concrete next step, and recent work moved it.
  • stalled We have tried, and we say what stopped us.
  • out of current scope The mathematics cannot reach it yet, and we say why.

The starting point

The God question making progress

What limits did an unlimited Universal Intelligence impose on itself, by what mechanics, and why?

Approach
Start again from the three-sentence core alone. Ask which limits would generate reality as it stands, using only derivations that are already established in physics and mathematics. Then compare with a story built without HMR.
Progress
The round 3 council produced a candidate set of limits and a list of what it reaches and what it does not. See the result below.
Limits
The round 3 work files were lost when the computer was replaced. Only the summary remains, so the tests need to be run again before anything is relied on.

A perspective before space and time making progress

To explain anything, a perspective must be established first. How can one exist without being oriented in space and time?

Approach
Look for the least a perspective needs. Look in established physics for a derivation of time from something that is not time.
Progress
Round 3 answers that a split into me and not me is already a perspective, and that it needs only difference and relation. It also points to the thermal time work of Connes and Rovelli (1994), which derives a flow of time from a state on an algebra of observables. The theorem behind it is established. Its physical reading is their hypothesis.

Round 3 council result, November 23, 2026

The council was asked for HMR's starting point and what follows from it. This is its summary. The work files were lost and this summary is what remains. Citations in this section are as the council gave them. We have not reopened those sources for this page.

The starting point

  • With no limits nothing is ruled out, so there are no facts.
  • A limit that stays imposed (applying it twice is the same as applying it once) splits the whole into me and not me. The council reports this as derived, not assumed.
  • A split is a perspective. It needs only difference and relation, not space or time.
  • Any part that models the whole is incomplete (Lawvere).
  • A suggestion: self-limiting and taking a perspective may be one act.

The most promising set of limits

  1. Me and not me.
  2. Finite information for each perspective, while new questions always stay possible (Rovelli's two postulates).
  3. Forgetting that belongs to perspectives, while the whole forgets nothing.
  4. The whole can be known from its parts together, and change is continuous and reversible (Hardy; Müller and Masanes, who obtain three dimensions).

What that set reaches, by established derivations

  • Quantum interference.
  • A finite speed, once neighbors exist (Lieb and Robinson).
  • Gravity as an equation of state (Jacobson, 1995).
  • Three dimensions of space: a candidate only.
  • A finite speed and the Planck minimum look like consequences and not inputs. A smallest length clashes with relativity. A largest count does not.

Tests the council ran

  • Distance counted in distinguishable steps puts the path from no to yes at π (Wootters), and coordinates that are square roots of chances give amplitudes.
  • One yes or no question has a state space of dimension 1, 2, 3, or 5 for real, signed, phase, or quaternion numbers. Only 3 can be known from its parts.
  • The drift of Version 1's dimension was reproduced (3.70, 4.53, 5.36).
  • A cap on direct parents plus a "patient" rule that pairs siblings grew an exact two-dimensional grid. It is fragile, so it is a diagnosis and not a model.
  • A finite signal cone appeared with links between neighbors only.
  • A reversible ring looked like decay to an observer who sees one number.

What cuts against HMR

  • A bounded number of links conflicts with Lorentz invariance (Bombelli, Henson, and Sorkin). This hits the growth mathematics of Versions 1 and 2. It does not hit the premises.
  • "Intelligence" does no work in the mathematics yet.
  • The second narrative, the one built with HMR, adds a motive and an order. It does not add a new equation.
  • Unfilled: the constants, particles, the origin of life, and why the limiting happened.

Five suggestions, awaiting a decision

None of these is adopted. Changes to HMR are its author's to accept.

  1. Make the irreducible unit one distinction, not one distance.
  2. Read "before the Big Bang" as "underlying".
  3. Make nearness the next open slot, starting from the clue that things used together become near.
  4. Choose a growth path: either cap "built on", or drop bounded links and use possible thoughts that carry weights and phases.
  5. Say in mathematics what "intelligence" adds, for example attention and liking.

The three that most else waits on

The project's own audit ranked open items by how many relations among HMR's claims each would join if it were settled. These three give most of the gain.

A rule of growth that yields space stalled

No rule tested grows a space or holds a number of dimensions. Why three dimensions: no answer.

Approach
Search rules by computer and measure the dimension from the share of related pairs. Success is a dimension near 4 holding steady over at least a hundredfold range of sizes, at 100,000 thoughts or more, or a theorem.
Progress
Nineteen rules and settings tested, none settles. A liking for the right balance at every scale closed only 10% to 23% of the gap in four dimensions. Two requirements were learned: the rule's focus must have no fixed limit, and what a new thought attends to must depend on which earlier thoughts are near one another.
Why stalled
For rules within ordinary probability this is a published open problem, and those who posed it expect the answer to need quantum theory. An established theorem already rules out the first family. Round 3 adds that any bounded number of links conflicts with Lorentz invariance.
New lead
A proposal to test: parents chosen by recency plus a weight for being used together, so that nearness emerges from use. A second reading to test, from tensegrity: local contact with global tension. The proposed source of three dimensions, that 3D is 2D plus the idea of distance and that distance needs an origin point, has no mathematics yet.

A memory that sits in one place making progress

Memory, a lasting particle, and "about" turn out to be one requirement: the law must favor the return of a shape.

Approach
Build rules that can see shape, borrowing from computing and AI, and measure whether late shape remembers early shape, how long it lasts, and whether it can be rewritten.
Progress
Proved: the first family has no memory at all. Found: rules that copy a shape remember strongly (agreement of about 0.4 to above 0.9 between early and late counts), and shapes run on in lines of descent. One rule gave a true two-way bit, but one for the whole pattern, kept by constant refresh.
Next step
A local store that is refreshed: growth that returns to the same neighborhood at a steady rate. A proposal to test alongside it: rewriting is reweighting and not deleting, a bit is a loop that renews itself, and new loops outvote old ones locally.
Limits
Every copy rule built so far breaks two of the four conditions of the growth family. Most rules were run at one or two settings.

A quantum rule for births making progress

Ordinary chance cannot link far-apart outcomes as strongly as experiments show. UI's rule must sit one rung above chance.

Approach
Let the numbers in the growth rule be complex. Require that the rule stays well defined at every size and keeps its phases after a closed moment. Then test linked outcomes: the score must come out above 2 and no higher than 2.83.
Progress
With a complex strength, pairs of histories interfere and triples do not. That toy rule is not well defined at large size. Version 2 proves that only the geometric part of a rule's weights survives a closed moment, which leaves one named family to test.
Next step
Test that family. A second proposal to test: all possible thoughts are born as potentials with phases, and attention selects among them, so that choosing is not a birth.
Limits
The survival proof is by hand and not yet checked by script.

The mechanics of thought

Where "about" comes from making progress

Everything about truth rests on one thought being about another. The one relation the model has does not pick out what a thought is about.

Approach
Two routes. Add "about" as a second relation. Or derive it: a later pattern is a record of an earlier one when its shape carries information about the earlier shape, which needs a law that remembers.
Progress
Copy rules give memory, so the second route is now possible in kind. Neither route is settled.
Proposed wording
The relation words themselves are open. One proposal: say "comes from" in place of "builds on" (B could not have arisen as it did without A), and say "models" in place of "about" (B carries A's shape). The test is whether "models" reduces to "comes from, with shape kept".

Levels, from one thought to a human mind stalled

A human thought is not one thought of UI.

Approach
Group thoughts into blocks and ask what carries upward.
Progress
The order carries upward when a block counts as before another only if all of it is.
Why stalled
The law is not known to carry, and in general a coarse law gains memory. Until this is settled, everything said about human minds is analogy.

UI's own liking, and HMR's values in the mathematics stalled

What UI likes is unknown in the mathematics, so "good" cannot be computed.

Approach
Write liking as a weight on patterns and look for the weight that reproduces the world.
Progress
One fact is known: liking cannot be "more relations", because the pattern would close to a single line, and the world holds only about one pair in ten related.
Why stalled
No candidate weight exists. Round 3 puts the same point more sharply: "intelligence" does no work in the mathematics yet.

A part with its own liking stalled

Which thoughts belong to one mind, and what that mind likes, are not defined. Self-deception cannot be defined without it.

Approach
One candidate: a part's liking is UI's liking averaged over what the part cannot see, so agreement with UI would rise with knowledge.
Progress
None beyond the candidate.
Why stalled
It waits on a definition of a part, which waits on levels.

Emotion stalled

Version 2 proposes that a feeling is the change in the logarithm of liking on one step. The proposal is open, and it is not accepted into HMR.

Approach
Four numbers on a step of thinking: feeling, mood, gain, and surprise.
Progress
One exact identity ties them together, checked numerically.
Why stalled
Four numbers will not tell grief from fear, so the named emotions are not derived. Whether emotion has its own mathematics or is a relation to thought is still an open question. The account also stops where weights are no longer ordinary chances.

Approximate truth stalled

As defined, truth is yes or no, and every physical law is an approximation.

Approach
Write a tolerance and a range into each claim.
Progress
Ranges are in the definitions. Tolerances and degrees of belief are absent.
Why stalled
Not yet worked on.

Meaning beyond order stalled

Content is only which thoughts rest on which. Qualities, "not", and laws about all cases have no form. "The sky is blue" cannot be written.

Approach
None yet.
Progress
None.
Why stalled
Not yet worked on.

A first number, and the size of an idea making progress

The first number this mathematics can produce is about HMR itself: its own graph of claims.

Approach
Cut HMR's pages into claims and links, mark each link certified, asserted, or open, and count the related pairs.
Progress
A first graph exists: 250 claims and 382 links, with about 1,160 certified relations among 181 claims. Its findings include that the first premise currently certifies nothing, and that HMR is a tree with some bracing and not a line. One coder, one pass, so only orders of magnitude should be trusted. Files are in the HMR Math repository.
Next step
Split the measure in two. Size (how large an idea is) should be measured separately from quality (how good it is), and proof belongs to quality. The current count mixes them. Candidate for size: weighted reach, with no proof required. Candidate for quality: coherence, evidence, testability, usefulness, and fit with HMR's values.

Physics

Inflation from a closed moment stalled

HMR conjectures that the Big Bang is a closed moment and inflation is growth after it.

Approach
Find a rule with steady exponential growth and an end, then match its rate, length, and ripples to the sky.
Progress
A rule with steady exponential growth exists: when each new thought builds on K earlier ones, the count side by side multiplies at about 0.4/K per step.
Why stalled
What grows in that rule has no dimension, so it is not space, and no larger K can fix it. This waits on a rule that yields space. After that it would still need a rate below 8 × 10−6 per step, 60 e-folds or more, and ripples of the measured tilt (0.965) and size (2.1 × 10−9).

Which form gravity takes out of current scope

HMR conjectures that gravity is not a field to quantize. The conjecture has two forms.

Approach
Form 1: the order itself is undecided between records, and two small masses can become entangled through gravity alone. Form 2: the order is definite and something rides on it, with no such entanglement and a jitter in geometry in its place.
Progress
Either form avoids the clash between relativity and quantum theory.
Why out of scope
A proposed laboratory experiment decides between the forms, and it is not ours to run. The tests of the conjecture itself cannot be run until a rule grows space.

The bit, derived stalled

The size of the first distance rests on each crossing relation carrying exactly one bit, and on one choice of which relations to count.

Approach
Derive both from the rule of growth. Then 4.34 thoughts per Planck volume becomes a prediction that can be tested.
Progress
None. With other reasonable counts the unit lands anywhere from about 0.7 to 3 Planck lengths.
Why stalled
There is no rule of growth to derive it from.

A number for coherence stalled

One quantity that rises when matter clumps and also ranks a living thing above a hot gas, on test cases fixed in advance.

Approach
None settled.
Progress
None.
Why stalled
Not yet worked on.

Particles, charge, spin, and one pure number out of current scope

The target is a lasting pattern with a period that can be computed, and a ratio of two periods equal to a measured mass ratio: 206.768 for the muon and electron, or 1836.153 for the proton and electron.

Approach
A particle would be a shape in the order that lasts and recurs.
Progress
Copy rules give lines of descent, a first lasting shape. A period has not been measured.
Why out of scope
It needs a memory in one place and a rule that grows space first. Nothing yet plays the part of charge, and spin has one lead that is not built.

Dark matter out of current scope

Approach
Ask where the count-based law of gravity stops holding. At very small accelerations one of its assumptions fails on its own terms.
Progress
A place to look, not a result.
Why out of scope
HMR has no mechanism, and none can be built until gravity comes from a grown pattern.

The constants, the origin of life, and why the limiting happened out of current scope

Approach
For life, HMR proposes an inversion: parts of the universe that carry a working model of themselves. For the constants, one clue: the free numbers of the growth rule change after a closed moment and settle toward fixed values.
Progress
None in the mathematics. Round 3 lists all of these as unfilled.
Why out of scope
Each needs a definition the mathematics does not have: a part that models itself, a lasting pattern, or a reason for the first limit.

The famous open problems of mathematics out of current scope

Approach
HMR takes a position on what mathematics is, and reads from it which way each problem should fall.
Progress
None. Version 2 moved none of the seven prize problems.
Why out of scope
The predictions are analogies from HMR's premises. None has a chain of proof behind it.
