Glossary

The words, defined

Each term in one or two plain sentences. Definitions marked as proposed belong to Version 2 and are not yet accepted into HMR.

Two that are easy to mix up

Thought

One committed act. It happens once, and it is permanent.

Idea

A set of claims, a matching to what they are about, and the range in which they are claimed. An idea can occur many times and in many minds.

A second pair: an idea is one connected pattern of claims. A discipline is several ideas that cannot be joined.

All terms

ABCDFGHIKLMNOPRSTUVW
About
In Version 2, a thought can be about another thought. Written as a map: m is about t. Whether this is a second relation or something derived from a law with memory is open. A proposed replacement word is "models".
Apart
Two thoughts are apart when neither came before the other. Their order of birth has no meaning. Space is what thoughts that are apart are to one another.
Attention
The set S of existing thoughts chosen at a birth. Calling it attention is an HMR reading.
Birth
The one operation of HMR math. Attend to a set of existing thoughts, and a new thought is born after all of them and after everything they rest on.
Braced
An idea is braced when its key relations are carried by more than one chain. One false link then costs only itself.
Builds on
The one relation of the model: each new thought builds on some of the thoughts before it. Also written "came before" and "rests on". A proposed replacement is "comes from".
Chain
A run of thoughts in which each came before the next. The longest chain between two thoughts is the time between them.
Closed moment
See Post.
Commit
The birth of a thought, seen as a permanent act. Before a commit there is an open stage of candidates. HMR conjectures that a measurement in physics is a commit that makes a record.
Consistent
An idea is consistent when some possible pattern could satisfy all of its claims at once.
Copy rule
A growth rule in which a new thought is more likely to take a shape that already exists. Copy rules are the first rules found that remember.
Cost
A number given to a pattern so that its chance is e to the minus cost. In the simplest rule: links times ln(1/p), plus pairs apart times ln(1/(1 - p)).
Discipline
Several ideas that cannot be joined into one. Contrast with Idea. By this definition physics as a whole is a discipline, because relativity and quantum theory cannot be joined as frameworks.
Faithful
A truthful idea that also misses nothing: every relation in what it is about appears in the idea.
Fan
One thought with many separate thoughts resting on it, none related to each other. In HMR's own mechanics the first boundary is a fan.
Feeling
Proposed in Version 2: how much the thought just committed changed liking. Open, and not accepted into HMR.
First distance
The distance between me and not me, the unit of one. In HMR physics it works out to about 0.69 Planck lengths under three assumptions.
Focus
The size of the set attended to at a birth, and how much weight each size is given.
Gain
Proposed in Version 2: how strictly choice follows liking. Version 1 called it the inverse of temperature.
God question
Explain and define the mechanics by which an unlimited Universal Intelligence created a reality in which it is limited.
History
One order in which the thoughts of a pattern could have been born. A pattern is a history with the order of unrelated births forgotten.
HMR
The Holistic Model of Reality. Its home is universemodel.ai.
Idea
A set of claims, a matching from those claims to what they are about, and the range in which they are claimed. A thought is one act; an idea can occur many times. The same idea can be true of one thing and false of another.
Ignorance
A blank: a thought about nothing the thinker can match. It is undecided, not false.
Insight
One thought that rests on two groups that were apart. It joins every thought of one to every thought of the other in a single step.
K rule
A growth rule in which each new thought builds on K earlier thoughts, chosen evenly from everything thought so far. It grows exponentially and holds no dimension.
Level
A way of looking at which a group of thoughts counts as one thought. Carrying the mathematics from one level to the next is an open problem.
Lie
The teller holds a belief, a different thing it says, and the knowledge that the two differ. A lie is strictly larger than the truthful report.
Liking
The weight w a rule gives a pattern, read as how much UI likes it. The chance of a step is a reference chance times w(next) / w(now). What UI's own liking is, nobody knows.
Limit
What an unlimited UI imposed on itself. Which limits, and why, is the God question.
Link
A direct relation: one thought came before another with nothing in between.
Live set
The bounded set of thoughts a part of the pattern can reach directly.
Me and not me
The first distinction. In HMR, the first thing UI distinguishes.
Mistake, honest
A belief with the wrong shape, held by a thinker with no thought that says so.
Mood
Proposed in Version 2: the feeling expected from the next thought.
n
The number of thoughts in a pattern.
Open stage
The set of candidate next thoughts before a birth, each with a weight. "Still processing".
Pattern
A finite set of thoughts with the relation "came before". Also called a state. In ordinary mathematics, a finite partial order.
Perspective
What is visible from a thought. In the round 3 result, a split into me and not me is already a perspective.
Post
A thought that every earlier thought comes before and every later thought comes after. Also called a closed moment. HMR conjectures the Big Bang was one.
Prime pattern
A pattern that is not a product of two smaller patterns.
Process
The law, together with the possible next thoughts that were not born. The process holds more than its trace does.
R
The number of relations in a pattern, links and implied relations together.
Range
The domain in which a claim is made. Two ideas whose roots clash may not clash inside the ranges where each was tested.
Recall
A new thought about an old one. It is never a replay, so it can be true or false.
Simplest rule
The growth rule in which each existing thought is attended to with the same chance p, independently. It makes the family concrete and is not offered as UI's law.
Slip
A report that departs from the belief without the teller knowing it.
State
See Pattern.
Surprise
How unexpected a thought was: minus the logarithm of the chance of that birth.
Suspect
A claim that belongs to a smallest set of claims that cannot all hold. In a contradiction, warrant is lost above every suspect.
Thought
The one element of HMR math: one committed act. It has no place and no size in space, only relations to other thoughts. "One thought" means one at a given level of looking.
Trace
The permanent record an act leaves. The pattern of thoughts is the trace of the process.
Truthful
A thought is truthful when what it is about exists and the thought sits in the same order as what it is about. Proposed in Version 2.
Undecided
A thought whose subject is not yet born, or that is about nothing in particular. A prediction is undecided until its subject is born.
Universal Intelligence (UI)
What HMR's premise says exists. It is not defined at the start: the premise says only, let it exist, without limits.
Untruthful
A thought whose subject exists and whose order does not match it.
Version 1 and Version 2
Version 1 (November 20, 2026) described thoughts and how they pile up. Version 2 (November 23, 2026) adds a mechanics of thinking: truth, mistakes, lies, emotion, and two negative results.
Void
The state with no thoughts in it. Growth starts here.
w, m, f
The sizes of one thought: w is how many thoughts it rests on in all, m is how many it attends to directly, and f is how many come to rest on it. The letter w is also used for liking, as on the live math page.
Wall
On this site, a place where nothing works yet.
Warranted
A thought is warranted when everything it rests on is truthful, or when it was checked directly against what it is about.
