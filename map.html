Turning the idea into objects

The step where words become mathematics. It is a choice, and a different choice would give a different mathematics.

  1. The AI team writes the idea as six assumptions: one intelligence, one element, one relation, only the pattern counts, no reaching across, and liking.

    AI guessingDetails: Assumptions, part C

  2. With those assumptions a state of UI is a finite set of thoughts ordered by "came before", an object mathematics already knows well: a finite partial order.

    AI derivation, checkedDetails: Primer, chapter 2

  3. Every finite pattern can be grown from nothing by births, and the counts of patterns (1, 1, 2, 5, 16, 63, 318) match the known ones.

    AI derivation, checkedDetails: Primer, chapter 3

  4. Patterns combine by four operators (beside, then, inside, paired), and putting one pattern after another creates n × n new relations.

    AI derivation, checkedDetails: Primer, chapter 6

The rule for the next thought

Here the mathematics lands on published physics.

  1. Four plain conditions force the rule for the next thought into one exact family, the same one published by Rideout and Sorkin in 1999.

    AI derivation, checkedDetails: Primer, chapter 7

  2. Attraction is already inside the simplest rule: the more that rests on a thought, the more likely the next thought builds on it.

    AI derivation, checkedDetails: Primer, chapter 8

  3. The weight a rule gives a pattern is read as how much UI likes it, and its logarithm as the action of physics.

    AI guessingDetails: Primer, chapter 9

  4. No rule of that family grows space: an established theorem says large patterns grown this way have time and no spatial structure.

    wallDetails: Open problem: space

  5. The family also cannot remember: later shape is independent of earlier shape, proved by hand and confirmed in 200,000 runs.

The mechanics of thinking (Version 2)

Truth, mistakes, lies, and emotion. The definitions are proposals. What follows from them is checked.

  1. Version 2 supposes a thought can be about another thought, and defines idea, truthful, untruthful, undecided, mistake, and lie from that.

    AI guessingDetails: Primer, chapter 10

  2. Given those definitions, a false thought removes the warrant of exactly itself and everything resting on it, checked on 708,178 cases.

Toward quantum theory

One rung above ordinary chance.

  1. When the strength in the simplest rule is made a complex number, pairs of histories interfere and triples do not, which is the signature of quantum theory.

    AI derivation, checkedDetails: HMR physics

  2. After a closed moment only the geometric part of a rule's weights survives, which leaves one named family of quantum rules to test.

Toward physics

Everything here holds only if a large pattern is a space, which no rule delivers yet.

  1. If a large pattern is a space, twelve statements of HMR match published physics, eight of them as theorems.

    AI derivation, checkedDetails: HMR physics

  2. Given three assumptions, the first distance works out to 0.693 Planck lengths; this trades one constant for another and predicts nothing.

    AI guessingDetails: HMR physics

  3. Walking a road physicists already know (Jacobson, 1995), counting the relations that cross a boundary leads to Einstein's equations, with three steps still open.

    AI derivation, checkedDetails: HMR physics

  4. Gravity may not be the kind of thing that is quantized: it would be how the order behaves on average, as pressure is how a gas behaves.

    AI guessingDetails: HMR physics

  5. The Big Bang is read as a closed moment and inflation as growth after it; no rule stands behind either reading.

    AI guessingDetails: Open problem: inflation

  6. Dark energy is read as the wobble in a count of thoughts; the formula and its size are causal set theory's, not HMR's.

    AI guessingDetails: HMR physics

  7. Particles, charge, spin, mass ratios, and the constants of nature: nothing is derived.

    wallDetails: Open problem: particles

  8. The origin of life, and why the limiting happened at all, are unfilled.

    wallDetails: Out of scope for now

What the map shows

  • The human part is short and sits entirely at the top. It is the premise and a set of directions.
  • The handoff is a single step: the choice to write the idea as six assumptions about thoughts and one relation. Everything below depends on that choice.
  • Most checked work is mathematics about order. Much of it restates published results, which is a good sign for the footing and no sign yet that HMR adds something physics lacks.
  • The walls cluster in three places: a rule that grows space, a memory in one place, and a quantum rule. The open problems page ranks them.
