Assumptions

What is assumed

Every theory starts from a few things it takes to be true. These are ours, in the order they enter. If one of them is wrong, everything resting on it has to be redone.

How to read the tags. premise is the core of HMR. HMR idea is the human idea, held loosely. modeling choice is a choice made by the AI team when turning the idea into mathematics. borrowed is taken from established physics or mathematics.

A. The premise

Three sentences. They are the only premises HMR commits to.

  1. A Universal Intelligence (UI) exists. premise
  2. Before the Big Bang it was unlimited. premise
  3. The Big Bang is the manifestation of a system in which that Universal Intelligence imposes limits on itself. premise

UI is not defined at this stage. The premise says only: let it exist, without limits.

B. The working claim of this site

  1. If a Universal Intelligence exists, it can be reverse engineered from the reality it produced. HMR idea
  2. To explain anything, a perspective must be established first. HMR idea
  3. The mathematics that physics has confirmed, and its measurements, are evidence to be matched. Its frameworks and concepts are clues and are not binding. HMR idea

C. The six assumptions of the mathematics

These turn the idea into something that can be calculated. They are stated on the live math page. The wording of the ideas is HMR's. The choice to write them this way is a modeling choice, and a different choice would give a different mathematics.

#NameWhat it saysKind
7One intelligenceReality is the thinking of one Universal Intelligence.premise
8One elementThe element is a thought. A thought has no place and no size. All it has is how it relates to other thoughts.modeling choice
9One relationThoughts happen one at a time, and each new thought builds on some of the thoughts before it. "Builds on" is the only relation. It never runs in a circle, and a thought rests on a finite number of others.modeling choice
10Only the pattern countsIf two thoughts are unrelated, it makes no difference which of them came first.modeling choice
11No reaching acrossWhat is thought next depends on what it builds on, and not on thoughts unrelated to it.modeling choice
12LikingEvery possible next thought has some chance, and the chances are UI's liking: it does more of what it likes.HMR idea

Two consequences follow at once. Time is the order of UI's thought. Space is what unrelated thoughts are to one another.

E. What HMR physics adds

The physics needs more than the mathematics has earned so far. Each of these is named on the live physics page where it enters.

#What it saysKind

F. What is not assumed

  • Space. It has to come out of the order of thoughts.
  • A background time. Unrelated thoughts have no before or after.
  • A finished infinity. At any moment the thoughts so far are a finite number.
  • Particles, forces, or constants. None is put in, and none has come out yet.

G. A known conflict between two of these

Assumptions 9 to 13 force a specific family of growth rules. Assumption 18 says a large pattern looks like space and time. An established theorem (Brightwell and Georgiou, 2010, as cited on the math page) says no rule of that family grows such a pattern. So the two cannot both hold as written.

The live pages handle this with ranges: the growth family is called a template and not UI's law, and the route to gravity is marked as holding only if the order is a space. The project's own audit found 32 claims resting on the growth family, 26 on the space assumption, and none on both. See the open problem.
