HMR physics

The same mechanics, set beside physics

HMR keeps every equation physics has confirmed. What it changes is the framework they are read in: space, time, and matter are not the bottom layer, Universal Intelligence is. This page maps what that has produced so far, and what it has not.

Where HMR physics stands

What it has

A unit. Twelve matches with published physics. A complete chain of equations to Einstein's gravity, borrowed from a known road. Two negative results that say exactly what the law lacks. Rules that remember.

What it lacks

A rule that grows space. A memory in one place. A particle. Any number that physics does not already have. Any confirmed prediction of its own.

The condition on everything

Nearly every physical statement here begins "if a large pattern of thoughts is shaped like space and time". No rule yet makes that true.

HMR physics adds its own assumptions to those of the mathematics. They are listed on the assumptions page.

What one thought is worth

The two negative results

The first rules cannot remember

In every rule of the first growth family, the shape of what is thought later is independent of the shape of what was thought earlier. Proved by hand. In 200,000 runs of 24 thoughts the correlation between early and late shape was 0.002 and 0.000, against a noise level of 0.002.

That rules out memory, a recurring idea, a particle, and "about", all at once. They are one requirement: the law must favor the return of a shape.

checked

No tested rule holds a dimension

In the rule where each thought builds on K earlier ones, the measured dimension passes through 4 and keeps rising: 3.82, 4.60, 5.37, 6.15 for K = 2 at 500 to 32,000 thoughts. A true four-dimensional scatter measures 4.0 at every size.

It had to fail. In a scatter with no preferred direction every thought has an unlimited number of direct relations, and this rule allows at most K.

checked Nineteen rules and settings in all. Confidence is moderate: three to five seeds, and most rules at one or two settings.

One positive: copy rules remember

When a new thought is more likely to take a shape that already exists, memory appears. Agreement between early and late counts of a shape ran from about 0.4 to above 0.9. Shapes carry themselves forward in lines of descent. One rule gave a two-way bit with a threshold, but it is one store for the whole pattern and sits in no place. Every copy rule still drifts in dimension, and every one breaks two of the four conditions. Memory has arrived. Space has not. numerical

The road to quantum mechanics

  • Quantum theory sits one rung above ordinary chance: two paths interfere, and three paths add nothing new. established
  • Make the strength p in the simplest rule a complex number, and pairs of histories interfere while triples do not. This rule is a toy: it is not well defined at large size. exact
  • On the standard test of linked outcomes, ordinary chance scores at most 2, a rule one rung up scores at most 2.83, and nature does not exceed that. established
  • After a closed moment only the geometric part of a rule's weights survives. One named family is left to test. exact Proved by hand, not yet checked by script.
  • A quantum rule for births: open.

The route to gravity

This walks a road physicists already know, the thermodynamics of spacetime (Jacobson, 1995), with pieces of causal set theory, and shows where HMR's first distance fits. It invents no new physics.

The route reproduces known gravity. It does not go beyond it. What remains is the wall every theory of quantum gravity stands in front of.

Is gravity the kind of thing that is quantized?

In HMR math there are two kinds of object. A pattern inside the order is counted: it occurs zero times, once, twice. The order itself is not counted, and its properties are averages over enormous numbers of thoughts. HMR conjecture

The conjecture: the forces are patterns within the order, which is why they come in quanta. Gravity is how the order behaves on average, as pressure is how a gas behaves. Quantizing Einstein's equations would then be like quantizing the gas law.

Form 1

The order itself is undecided between records. It predicts that two small masses can become entangled through gravity alone.

Form 2

The order is definite, and what behaves in a quantum way rides on it. It predicts no such entanglement, and a jitter in geometry in its place. It asks for an inner state on a thought, which HMR math does not have.

A proposed laboratory experiment decides between them. Which form holds is open.

The clash between relativity and quantum theory, as three claims

  1. Geometry is definite.
  2. Geometry follows matter.
  3. Matter can be in two places at once, at every size.

Any two can hold together. All three cannot. The measurements do not conflict, because the claims have not been tested together: more than eighteen powers of ten in mass lie between the heaviest object seen in two places at once and the lightest source whose gravity has been measured. The frameworks conflict. HMR starts from thoughts and their order, so it does not have to hold all three, and each form above gives one up.
